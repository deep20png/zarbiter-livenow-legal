Privacy Policy for Zarbiter - Live Now

Last Updated: October 2026

Zarbiter ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Zarbiter - Live Now mobile application (the "Service").

Our app requires access to your device's Camera and Microphone to record and stream video. This data is sent directly to the RTMP server (e.g., YouTube, Facebook, Twitch) you choose. We do not record, store, or transmit your video/audio to our own servers.

We do not store your live video streams on our servers. Stream keys are stored locally on your device.
